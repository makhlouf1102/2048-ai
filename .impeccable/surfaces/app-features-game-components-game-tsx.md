## Scope and mode

The single 2048 game route is an Operate surface.

## Audience, job, and action

Casual players play manually or hand the same board to Milo. The two primary actions are “Let Milo play” and “Mix a new board.”

## Content and constraints

Preserve the game rules, manual controls, AI autoplay contract, and Milo as the named AI companion. AI telemetry may be simplified and renamed for casual comprehension.

## Chosen direction

Milo’s Painted Playroom treats the 4×4 board as a little geometric town assembled from saturated painted fields. The memorable moment is Milo’s directional brushstroke changing in the companion panel as the live board moves.
