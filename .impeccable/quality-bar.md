# Quality Bar: Milo’s Painted Playroom

The finished surface should feel as materially committed as a small gouache painting: pigment variation is visible at normal viewing size, color fields are decisive rather than pastel decoration, and every control belongs to the same outlined geometric vocabulary.

- The complete 4×4 board and Milo’s studio are simultaneously visible at 1440×1100.
- At 390×844, the complete board is visible and the beginning of Milo’s studio follows immediately below it; all prose keeps a readable measure.
- The board is the largest object, Milo is the second, and telemetry is supporting detail.
- The palette is saturated but operational states remain distinguishable by copy, shape, and position as well as color.
- Buttons feel painted and tactile, with fine ink outlines, soft physical depth, and unmistakable focus states.
- The generated gouache raster supplies material, never content: numbers, controls, and state remain semantic HTML and CSS.
- A first-time viewer should describe “the colorful number town with Milo beside it,” not “a dashboard with rounded cards.”
